About Streamscape.

Streamscape is a fictional streaming service used for a security-training exercise. It carries films, series, and music from a handful of made-up Pacific studios and labels.

01

How the service is put together

The public catalog you browse is drawn from the same dataset the partner console uses. On the public site, only released titles are shown. The partner console is meant to show each partner only their own titles' aggregate numbers.

02

For partners

Studios and labels who place titles with Streamscape can see how those titles perform through the partner console.
